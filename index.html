Dear Ynaroe,

I don't know if I always express it enough, but I am genuinely thankful that you came into my life. Of all the things that happened unexpectedly, meeting you is one of the things I would always be grateful for.

You have become a special part of my life. Even on ordinary days, your presence means something to me. You don't have to do anything extraordinary just to make me appreciate you. Sometimes, simply having you around is already enough to make my day better.

I know you have dreams of your own, things you want to achieve, and a future you're trying to build. I hope you never stop pursuing those dreams. I hope someday you'll get the success you've been working so hard for, and that all your sacrifices will be worth it.

I wish to see you grow, become the person you want to be, and experience the happiness you truly deserve. I hope life gives you opportunities that make you proud of yourself. And when you finally reach the things you've been praying for, I hope you take a moment to appreciate yourself because you deserve to celebrate how far you've come.

But please, even when things get difficult, don't ever think that you're alone. When you're tired of everything, when you feel like the world is too heavy, or when you think you can't do it anymore, I hope you remember that I'm here.

You don't always have to be strong. You don't always have to pretend that you're okay. You can rest, you can cry, you can tell me what's bothering you. You don't have to figure everything out by yourself.

I may not be able to solve every problem you face, but I can listen. I can stay. I can remind you that you are capable, that you are worthy, and that I believe in you even on the days when you struggle to believe in yourself.

Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for the memories, the laughter, the moments, and even the little things that probably seem ordinary to you but mean so much to me. Thank you for being the person you are.

I hope your future is filled with beautiful things. I hope you become successful, happy, peaceful, and fulfilled. And while you're making your way through life, I hope you never forget that somewhere along the way, you have me, cheering for you and wishing you the best.

I'm proud of you, even for the things you think are too small to be proud of.

With all my love,

Someone who will always believe in you. ♡
